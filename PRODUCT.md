# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React and Vite with plain CSS. The user approved a code-first workflow.

## Users

Prospective customers evaluating a social-media marketing agency and looking for evidence of strategy, creative capability, services, pricing, and team credibility.

## Product Purpose

Viralup presents a social-media growth agency and guides visitors from the core promise through process, work, services, pricing, team, testimonials, frequently asked questions, and contact actions.

## Positioning

The page pairs performance-oriented agency messaging with a fashion-forward stream of real campaign media, making the work itself the main proof.

## Operating Context

This is a responsive, scroll-led marketing landing page. The first draft must closely reproduce the supplied Viralup Framer reference at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page responsive site with anchor navigation.
- Working menu, pricing selector, FAQ accordion, and locally handled newsletter state.
- Reference media and fonts are packaged locally rather than hotlinked.
- No backend, external form submission, or CMS integration is in scope for this first draft.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Viralup name, blue growth-mark logo, copy, content sequence, light-gray canvas, white inset surfaces, blue accent, Inter Display and Instrument Serif pairing, and media-led presentation from the approved reference.
- The reference is a fidelity target, not an inspiration to reinterpret.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference: https://viralup.framer.website/
- Full-page capture: `work/viralup-reference-full.png`
- Source imagery, video, logo treatment, partner marks, and fonts: `public/assets/viralup/`
- Claims, pricing, names, and testimonials are template content copied from the supplied reference; they have not been independently verified.

## Product Principles

- Let real campaign media carry the visual identity.
- Keep the offer and primary action obvious in the first viewport.
- Preserve generous pacing while keeping every section purposeful.
- Match the supplied reference before adding invention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic regions, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, sufficient contrast, descriptive image text, reduced-motion support, and touch targets of at least 44px.
